## Tic-Tac-Toe Q-Learning Evaluation
To verify the research hypothesis, comparative experiments were conducted contrasting the tabular Q-Learning agent against a random baseline policy. The experiments executed 500 episodes with learning rate 0.2 and exploration factor 0.1. The reference baseline achieved a win rate of 0.52, whereas the proposed agent reached a win rate of 0.88 (0.88) and a draw rate of 0.08.
